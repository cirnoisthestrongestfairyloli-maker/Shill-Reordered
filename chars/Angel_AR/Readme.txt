=====<Move Details>=====

 - Red Sky of Yaponesia has a window of invincibility during active frames.
 - EX Red Sky of Yaponesia has projectile invincibility during startup as well.
 - EX Mad Murder Roulette has full body invincibility during active frames.
 - Mad Murder Roulette, Bye Bye Rouge, Shelter from Storm and Crown Under Sky can all be juggled afterwards with any attack;
   however, this can only be done once per combo.
 - Formalist's Blue, Train to See Cherry Blossoms, and Impotent Symptom are all low attacks.
 - Citizens of the World, Buggy and Coffin, With a Lamp for Pathway, and State of Heat Haze are all overhead attacks.
 - Mad Murder Roulette, Cosmic Futen Swing, and Lost Homeland are all command grabs.
 - Fullmoon Evening is unblockable. 
 - Omogatoki has a small window of invincibility during the twirl.
 - Winds Fairground and Blue Monday Parade are counter attacks performed with the same command:
   - When you counter ground attack, Winds Fairground comes out.
   - When you counter air attacks, Blue Monday Parade comes out.
 - Survivor's Banquet hits only OTG.

UNCHAINED CIRCLE SYSTEM:
Angel has her own sub-system of Special Moves, with four types of Unchain Circle moves:

 * Starters
 * Links
 * Specials
 * Finishers

 - Starters are the only ones that can be performed in neutral. They can be canceled into Links.
 - Links can be used after Starters and can be canceled into Specials, Finishers or other Links.
 - When canceling a Link into another Link, you can't use neither the same direction nor the same input that the current one uses.
   Example: if you perform Bye Bye Rouge (U + p), you can't go into none of the Links performed with U nor p.
 - Specials can be used after Links and can be canceled into Finishers.
 - Finishers can only be used after Links or Specials.
 - Beyond Frames is both an Unchained Circle Starter and an Unchained Circle Finisher.


=====<Gameplay Notes>=====

COMBO SYSTEM:
 - Some Normal attacks can be canceled into Command, Special and Super moves
 - Some Special moves can be canceled into Super moves
 - Some Lv1 Super moves can be canceled into MAX Super moves
 - Cancelling a Special, Super move or Custom Combo into a Super move resets the juggle points
   but also reduces its damage

CUSTOM COMBO:
 - Removes cancellable attack restrictions and gives you a lot of freedom to combo them
 - Juggle limit is lifted
 - You can only use EX Special or Super moves from the point the character starts flashing faster
   and brighter, and doing so ends Custom Combo
 - Gives you a short invulnerability window at the start
