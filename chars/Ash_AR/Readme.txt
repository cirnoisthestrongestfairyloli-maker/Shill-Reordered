=====<Mode Overview>=====

This character has two different modes:

<Ash>
- Regular Ash
- Based primarly on his XIII and XV movesets

<Evil Ash>
- An evil version of Ash unleashed by absorbing Saiki
- Based primarly on his XIII boss moveset



=====<.DEF Overview>=====

This char has five different .def files, here's what each one does:

<ASH.def>
The mode is selected via palette:

Palettes 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6	-> Normal Ash mode
Palettes 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12	-> Evil Ash mode

To add him to your Mugen, add the following line to your select.def, under [Characters]:
Ash,

<NORMALASH.def>
Only Normal Ash mode.
To add him to your Mugen, add the following line to your select.def, under [Characters]:
Ash/NormalAsh.def,

<EVILASH.def>
Only Evil Ash mode.
To add him to your Mugen, add the following line to your select.def, under [Characters]:
Ash/EvilAsh.def,


=====<Movelist>=====

 U - up          x - light punch        a - light kick
 D - down        y - medium punch       b - medium kick
 F - forward     z - heavy punch        c - heavy kick
 B - back        p - any punch          k - any kick
 s - start       2p- two punches        2k- two kicks

 (Air) - Move must be performed in the air.
 (EX)  - Move has an EX version, performed by pressing two punch/kick buttons.
 (MAX) - Use two punch/kick buttons when performing a Super move to power it up.

=====<Move Details>=====

 - HP Ventôse and EX Ventôse fire two projectiles.
 - MP Ventôse fires two projectiles. HP Ventôse fires four projectiles [Evil Ash].
 - EX Ventôse, Nivôse, Germinal Caprice and EX Génie cause soft knockdown.
 - EX Nivôse has full body invincibility on startup.
 - All versions of Nivôse have full body invincibility on startup [Evil Ash].
 - EX Germinal Caprice and EX Génie (2P) launch for a juggle reset.
 - MK Germinal Caprice launches for a juggle reset. HK Germinal Caprice crumples grounded opponents [Evil Ash].
 - EX Génie (2K) tracks the opponent's location.
 - Thermidor, Prairial, Sans-Culottes and Espoir cause hard knockdown.
 - Espoir fires five projectiles.
 - Ancien Régime tracks the opponent's location and has infinite juggle potential [Evil Ash].

SANS-CULOTTES:
 - Lasts 5 seconds.
 - All moves gain infinite juggle potential.
 - All special moves become command cancelable.
 - All command normals become special cancelable.
 - You can't build nor use meter while the buff is active.


=====<Treasure Hunter>=====

Upon using SANS-CULOTTES, this character has access to three secret Special Moves inspired by the Sacred Treasures Team: Germinal [Chizuru], Fructidor [Iori] and Frimaire [Kyo]. In this character's folder, there is a file called "Config.txt". Choose whether or not you want to be able to perform the aforementioned Special Moves by editing it like in the following example: 

[State 7000, 0 = Boss Mode Disabled, 1 = Boss Mode Enabled]
type = ParentVarSet
trigger1 = 1
var(57) = 0 ;CHANGE THIS LINE
 
This means that Ash will not be able to perform Germinal, Fructidor and Frimaire. To make him perform said moves, simply change the number to the right of "var(57) = " like in the following example: 
 
[State 7000, 0 = Boss Mode Disabled, 1 = Boss Mode Enabled]
type = ParentVarSet
trigger1 = 1
var(57) = 1 ;CHANGE THIS LINE



=====<Gameplay Notes>=====

COMBO SYSTEM:
 - Some Normal attacks can be canceled into Command, Special and Super moves
 - Some Special moves can be canceled into Super moves
 - Some Lv1 Super moves can be canceled into MAX Super moves
 - Cancelling a Special, Super move or Custom Combo into a Super move resets the juggle points
   but also reduces its damage

CUSTOM COMBO:
 - Removes cancellable attack restrictions and gives you a lot of freedom to combo them
 - Juggle limit is lifted
 - You can only use EX Special or Super moves from the point the character starts flashing faster
   and brighter, and doing so ends Custom Combo
 - Gives you a short invulnerability window at the start
