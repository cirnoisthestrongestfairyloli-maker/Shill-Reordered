Notes
==========================================================================================
-This character was made with only single and turns mode in mind, so using this in simul mode is probably not a good idea.

-It is recommended that you set the WavChannels to 64 or higher in mugen.cfg. This will prevent sound effects from cutting each other off.

-Due to the custom fall states, Stupa's Training character will not realize that they can recover despite "recover" being turned on. To fix this, Paste this code at the bottom of Training's config file:

[State 5050, 4] ;Recover near ground
type = SelfState
triggerall = stateno = 6050
triggerall = Vel Y > 0
triggerall = Pos Y >= -20
triggerall = alive
triggerall = CanRecover
trigger1 = Command = "recovery"
trigger2 = var(16) = 1
trigger2 = var(23)>=var(22)
value = 5200 ;HITFALL_RECOVER

[State -2]; Recover in mid air
type = selfState
triggerall = stateno = 6050
triggerall = Vel Y > -1
triggerall = alive
triggerall = CanRecover
trigger1 = Command = "recovery"
trigger2 = var(16) = 1
trigger2 = var(23)>=var(22)
value = 5210 ;HITFALL_AIRRECOVER

-Downloading this character from Mugen Guild or OHMSBY's Mugen site ensures that this is the latest version.

==========================================================================================
Introduction
==========================================================================================
Marx is a boss character in the same vein as Apocalypse in Marvel vs. Street Fighter or Takemikazuchi in Blazblue Chronophantasma. He is specifically designed to be controlled by the AI, although he is playable.
==========================================================================================

==========================================================================================
Movelist for player control
==========================================================================================
A: Cutter Blades

A+Down: Eye Dropper

B: Seeds

C: Ice Bomb

Y+Any Direction: Teleport

Z: Portal Crawl

A+B: Laser

B+C: Black Hole
==========================================================================================

==========================================================================================
Misc. Info
==========================================================================================
-After Marx loses a round, Marx will start using his Black Hole attack, each of his other attacks will be upgraded in some way, and he will generally become more aggressive. Under Player control, Marx can use his Black Hole even before losing a round.

-Black Hole is immediately followed up with Marx's Astral Heat if the opponent's health is low enough.

-The projectiles from Eye Dropper can be broken by just about any attack that isn't a throw

-Dealing enough damage to Marx while he is charging his laser will cause him to flinch.

-Hitting Marx with an Ultra Burst at any given time will immediately cause him to flinch.

-Marx has access to the Not Over Yet mechanic, which boosts the damage dealt by his Laser and Black Hole by 20% when active.

-Various aspects on Marx can be adjusted in his Config file to make him a more suitable challenge for your roster.
