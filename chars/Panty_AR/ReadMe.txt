:====:
:Info:
:====:

Panty is one of the two main protagonists of the Panty & Stocking with Garterbelt anime series by Gainax.
Panty is an angel, but due to her disruptive behavior, she was kicked out of heaven, just like her sister, 
Stocking. She is the older sister, and is represented as a 'slut', as she likes to have sex with many men, 
and unlike Stocking, she swears more than her and she hates sweets, but loves very spicy snacks. Her 
signature weapon she uses is the spiritual pistol, Backlace, using her panties to turn them into it. Her 
name is derived from the object she weaponizes. Just like Stocking, she hunts ghosts that threat Daten City 
so she can get backto heaven after collecting heaven coins when defeated, although she's not that interested 
in fighting ghosts and instead in sex. She was voiced by Arisa Ogasawara in the japanese dub, whereas in the 
english dub, she was voiced by Jamie Marchi.
