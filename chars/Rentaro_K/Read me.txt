Rentaro_K (Rentaro Satomi Arranged) by Kohaku for 1.0
======================================================

This is Rentaro from Black Bullet/Dengeki Bunko Fighting Climax. He is not accurate to the source game and instead plays to a custom style.

------------------------------------------------------
a = Any attack button (X/Y/Z)
aa = Press any two attack buttons simultaneously
aaa = Press all attack buttons simultaneously
(AWK) = Attack can only be performed while in Awakening
(SP) = Attack can utilize SP to further boost its effectiveness
(Air) = Attack can only be used in the air
(Air OK) = Attack can also be used in the air

Abilities
=========
[Wake Up Attack] - [Unebiko] or [Unebiko Ryuu] while getting up after being knocked down

[Guard Counter] - F+aa or B+aa (1000 power)(During a standing or crouching block)
AP Skill:
- During Awakening, 100 AP is required to use the attack instead of 1000 Power.

[Awaken] - A (100 AP)
AP Skill:
- 50 AP will be consumed
- 200 Power will be recovered (No power gain if you have 3 SP)
Spirit Point Recovery:
- Gain 1 SP upon use
----------------------------------------
Rentaro has access to a unique form of Awakening which grants him the ability to utilize Awakening Points (AP) and Spirit Points (SP) to enhance his capabilities in battle. Awakening also has the added benefit of increasing the damage of all your attacks by 10%.

Awakening is accessed through the use of AP and can only be activated with you have sufficient AP. Once enough AP has been gained, you can press "A" to activate Awakening mode which will immediately consume 50 AP upon activation (the initial burst can hit opponents for small damage, but with large hit stun). There is no time limit on the mode and Rentaro can stay in Awakening indefinitely as long as he has AP in his awakening gauge. With the exception of EX Drives and the Climax Drive, all attacks that land on the opponent will drain AP and once it drops to 0, you will automatically revert back to your normal state. To exit out of Awakening manually, simply press the "A" button again, however there is a 5 second cooldown whenever you enter the mode meaning you will have to wait till the cooldown passes before you can enter Awakening again. Exiting Awakening through this way will also leave you wide open, so pick your moment carefully. 

Outside of Awakening AP can be charged by inflicting damage with normal and special attacks as well as recieving damage. You can only charge AP by taking damage while Awakening is active.

Every time you enter Awakening you will gain 1 SP which is represented by the segmented meter above the AP gauge. Rentaro can stock up to a maximum of 3 SP at any give time. You will also be able gain bonus power every time you enter Awakening mode, however you will stop recieving the bonus power if you have a full SP meter (3 SP). SP can only be spent while in Awakening and is used to enhance a Special, EX Drive, or Climax Drive even further. The effects on attacks and the cost of SP varies between moves. 
----------------------------------------

[Overlimit] - Passive Ability
----------------------------------------
Rentaro has a unique trait which grants him the ability to use his AP as a means to use his EX Drives and Climax Drives in battle instead of Power.

With Overlimit his AP gauge essentially becomes a second power bar, acting as the main resource for EX Drives and Climax Drives while in Awakening. The drawback is that the cost to perform said moves will be increased when using AP. For example if an EX Drive that would normally cost 1000 power outside of Awakening will now cost 150 AP to use while Awakening is active and Climax Drives that costs 3000 power will now cost to 350 AP while in Awakening. Despite the hefty increase in cost, having access to 2 resources to access EX Drives and Climax Drives is incredibly versatile.

Overlimit also has a secondary effect of replinishing the other resource that wasn't used. For example, Magnum Shot will cost 1000 Power to perform outside of Awakening, but will have the added benefit of slightly recovering his AP. While in Awakening Magnum Shot changes to cost 150 AP to perform, but will recover his Power meter instead. Use this to your advantage to always have meter in any situation!

Special Moves
=============
[Gunfire] - D,F,a (Air OK)
- X version will fire straight
- Y version will fire downward
- Z version will fire upward
- Air version will always fire downwards

[Unebiko] - F,D,DF,a

[Kasen] - D,B,X
- Attack can be charged by holding down X 
- The attack will launch automatically when fully charged
- Attack range is increased at full charge
- Charge time is decreased if [Terminal Horizon] is active

[Uki Rouchou] - D,B,Y
- Attack can be charged by holding down Y 
- The attack will launch automatically when fully charged
- Increases run distance and knocks opponents up at full charge
- Charge time is decreased if [Terminal Horizon] is active

[Rokuro Kabuto] - D,B,Z
- Attack can be charged by holding down Z 
- The attack will launch automatically when fully charged
- Increases run distance and hits overhead at full charge
- Charge time is decreased if [Terminal Horizon] is active

[Shoukahanameishi] - D,B,a (Air)

[Enju - High Kick] - D,D,a (When assist gauge is full)
- Can only be used outside of Awakening

[Enju - Dive Kick] - D,D,a (AWK)(SP)(When assist gauge is full)
- Can only be used  while in Awakening
Spirit Point Usage:
- During Awakening, 1 SP will be consumed
- Enju will follow up with an additional dive kick
- Attack can still be used without SP 


EX Drive Attacks
================
[Magnum Shot] - D,F,aa (1000 Power)(SP)(Air OK)
Overlimit Skill:
- In Awakening, 150 AP will be consumed and 300 Power will be recovered
- Outside of Awakening, 1000 Power will be consumed and 50 AP will be recovered
Spirit Point Usage:
- During Awakening, 1 SP will be consumed
- Damage is increased by 20% and ignores damage dampener
- Attack can still be used without SP 

[Unebiko Ryuu] - F,D,DF,aa (1000 Power)
Overlimit Skill:
- In Awakening, 150 AP will be consumed and 300 Power will be recovered
- Outside of Awakening, 1000 Power will be consumed and 50 AP will be recovered

[Homura Kasen] - D,B,aa (1000 Power)(SP)
- Attack can be charged by holding down any attack button
- The attack will launch automatically when fully charged
- Attack range is increased at full charge
- Charge time is decreased if [Terminal Horizon] is active
Overlimit Skill:
- In Awakening, 150 AP will be consumed and 300 Power will be recovered
- Outside of Awakening, 1000 Power will be consumed and 50 AP will be recovered
Spirit Point Usage:
- During Awakening, 1 SP will be consumed
- A fully charged attack will cause a wall slam
- Attack can still be used without SP 

[Inzen Shoukahanameishi] - D,B,aa (1000 Power)(SP)(Air)
Overlimit Skill:
- In Awakening, 150 AP will be consumed and 300 Power will be recovered
- Outside of Awakening, 1000 Power will be consumed and 50 AP will be recovered
Spirit Point Usage:
- During Awakening, 1 SP will be consumed
- Attack will cause a floor slam
- Attack can still be used without SP 

Soul Trigger
============
[Terminal Horizon] - aaa (3 SP)
- Duration is increased when used while in Awakening
- Performing a Climax Drive while [Terminal Horizon] is active will extend its duration (can not exceed maximum)
Spirit Point Usage:
- Requires a full SP meter (3 SP) 
- 3 SP will be consumed
- Cancel any normal or special attack on the ground
- Temporarily gain an increase in movement speed
- Charge time of [Kasen], [Uki Rouchou], [Rokuro Kabuto], and [Homura Kasen] is decreased
- Cannot gain any SP while buff is active

Climax Drives
=============
[Unlimited Burst] - D,F,D,F,a (3000 Power)(SP)
Overlimit Skill:
- In Awakening, 350 AP will be consumed and 500 Power will be recovered
- Outside of Awakening, 3000 Power will be consumed and 75 AP will be recovered
Spirit Point Usage:
- During Awakening, a maximum of 3 SP will be consumed
- Damage is increased by 10% for each SP used
- Attack can still be used without SP 

[Final Strike] - D,B,D,B,a (3000 Power)
AP Skill Recovery:
- 100 AP will be recovered upon use
- AP is not recovered while in Awakening
