Shana_K (Shana Arranged) by Kohaku for 1.0
======================================================

This is Shana from Shakugan no Shana/Dengeki Bunko Fighting Climax. She is not accurate to the source game and instead plays to a custom style.

------------------------------------------------------
a = Any attack button (X/Y/Z)
aa = Press any two attack buttons simultaneously
aaa = Press all attack buttons simultaneously
(AWK) = Attack can only be performed while in Awakening.
(SP) = Attack can utilize SP to further boost its effectiveness.
(Air) = Attack can only be used in the air
(Air OK) = Attack can also be used in the air

Abilities
=========
[Air Dash] - F,F (Air)

[Guard Counter] - F+aa or B+aa (1000 power)(During a standing or crouching block)
AP Skill:
- During Awakening, 100 AP is required to use the attack instead of 1000 Power

[Awaken] - A (100 AP)
AP Skill:
- 50 AP will be consumed
- 200 Power will be recovered (No power gain if you have 3 SP)
Spirit Point Recovery:
- Gain 1 SP upon use
----------------------------------------
Shana has access to a unique form of Awakening which grants her the ability to utilize Awakening Points (AP) and Spirit Points (SP) to enhance her capabilities in battle. Awakening also has the added benefit of increasing the damage of all your attacks by 10%.

Awakening is accessed through the use of AP and can only be activated with you have sufficient AP. Once enough AP has been gained, you can press "A" to activate Awakening mode which will immediately consume 50 AP upon activation (the initial burst can hit opponents for small damage, but with large hit stun). There is no time limit on the mode and Shana can stay in Awakening indefinitely as long as she has AP in her awakening gauge. With the exception of EX Drives and the Climax Drive, all attacks that land on the opponent will drain AP and once it drops to 0, you will automatically revert back to your normal state. To exit out of Awakening manually, simply press the "A" button again, however there is a 3 second cooldown whenever you enter the mode meaning you will have to wait till the cooldown passes before you can enter Awakening again. Exiting Awakening through this way will also leave you wide open, so pick your moment carefully.

Outside of Awakening AP can be charged by inflicting damage with normal and special attacks as well as recieving damage. You can only charge AP by taking damage while Awakening is active.

Every time you enter Awakening you will gain 1 SP which is represented by the segmented meter above the AP gauge. Shana can stock up to a maximum of 3 SP at any give time. You will also be able gain bonus power every time you enter Awakening mode, however you will stop recieving the bonus power if you have a full SP meter (3 SP). SP can only be spent while in Awakening and is used to enhance a Special, EX Drive, or Climax Drive even further. The effects on attacks and the cost of SP varies between moves. 
----------------------------------------

[Overlimit] - Passive Ability
----------------------------------------
Shana has a unique trait which grants her the ability to use her AP as a means to use her EX Drives and Climax Drives in battle instead of Power.

With Overlimit her AP gauge essentially becomes a second power bar, acting as the main resource for EX Drives and Climax Drives while in Awakening. The drawback is that the cost to perform said moves will be increased when using AP. For example if an EX Drive that would normally cost 1000 power outside of Awakening will now cost 150 AP to use while Awakening is active and Climax Drives that costs 3000 power will now cost to 350 AP while in Awakening. Despite the hefty increase in cost, having access to 2 resources to access EX Drives and Climax Drives is incredibly versatile.

Overlimit also has a secondary effect of replinishing the other resource that wasn't used. For example, Crimson Lotus will cost 1000 Power to perform outside of Awakening, but will have the added benefit of slightly recovering her AP. While in Awakening Crimson Lotus changes to cost 150 AP to perform, but will recover her Power meter instead. Use this to your advantage to always have meter in any situation!

EX Drive Attacks
================
[Blaze Wing] - D,F,aa (1000 Power)(SP)(Air OK)
Overlimit Skill:
- In Awakening, 150 AP will be consumed and 300 Power will be recovered
- Outside of Awakening, 1000 Power will be consumed and 50 AP will be recovered
Spirit Point Usage:
- During Awakening, 1 SP will be consumed
- Maximum hits increased
- Attack can still be used without SP 

[Crimson Rose] - F,D,DF,aa (1000 Power)(SP)(Air OK)
Overlimit Skill:
- In Awakening, 150 AP will be consumed and 300 Power will be recovered
- Outside of Awakening, 1000 Power will be consumed and 50 AP will be recovered 
Spirit Point Usage:
- During Awakening, 1 SP will be consumed
- When used on the ground, the final slash will cause an explosion of fire that deals additional damage
- When used in the air, the final slash will throw a flaming wave down to the ground to cause an explosion
- Attack can still be used without SP 

[Scaldic Sword] - D,B,aa (1000 Power)(SP)
Overlimit Skill:
- In Awakening, 150 AP will be consumed and 300 Power will be recovered
- Outside of Awakening, 1000 Power will be consumed and 50 AP will be recovered
Spirit Point Usage:
- During Awakening, 1 SP will be consumed
- Last attack will generate a blast for more damage
- Attack can still be used without SP 

Soul Trigger
============
[Judgment] - aaa (3 SP)
- Duration is increased when used while in Awakening
- Performing a Climax Drive while [Judgment] is active will extend its duration (can not exceed maximum)
Spirit Point Usage:
- Requires a full SP meter (3 SP)
- 3 SP will be consumed
- Cancel any normal or special attack on the ground
- Recover 250 (500 in Awakening) Power upon activation 
  - Will recover 50 (100 in Awakening) AP instead if activated at full power
- Gradually gain more power over time 
  - Will start recovering AP instead when power is full
- Cannot gain any SP while buff is active

Climax Drives
=============
[Blazing Heart] - D,F,D,F,a (3000 Power)(SP)
Overlimit Skill:
- In Awakening, 350 AP will be consumed and 500 Power will be recovered
- Outside of Awakening, 3000 Power will be consumed and 75 AP will be recovered
Spirit Point Usage:
- During Awakening, a maximum of 3 SP will be consumed
- Damage is increased by 10% for each SP used
- Attack can still be used without SP 

[Infernal Blade] - D,B,D,B,a (3000 Power)
AP Skill Recovery:
- 100 AP will be recovered upon use
- AP is not recovered while in Awakening
