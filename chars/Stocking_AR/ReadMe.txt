:====:
:Info:
:====:

Stocking is one of the two main protagonists of the Panty & Stocking with Garterbelt anime series by Gainax.
Stocking is an angel who used to live on heaven along with Panty, but due to her disruptive behavior, she was kicked out of 
there. She is the younger sister, and is represented by the sin 'glutony', as she's obsessed to eat sweets, and unlike Panty, 
she's not very interested in sex and swears less often, is smarter than Panty and has more common sense, and hates spicy snacks. 
Her signature weapon are the spiritual Stripes, which are two katanas, using her stockings to turn them into them. Her name is 
derived from the object she weaponizes. Just like Panty, she hunts ghosts that threat Daten City so she can get back to heaven 
after collecting enough heaven coins when defeated. She emphatizes skill over power, contrary to Panty. She was voiced by 
Mariya Ise in the japanese dub, whereas in the english dub, she was voiced by Monica Rial.
