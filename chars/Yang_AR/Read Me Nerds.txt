Trait:   
Burst (C + attack) – While being in get-hit states, Yang can burst out of opponent’s combo. Doesn’t work against throws and hypers. Can only be used once per match.
Smart Combo – Work similiar to BBTag as Yang can auto combo by mashing Light or Medium buttons while standing.
Double Jump.
Reversal Action: Strawberry Sunrise (C) – Has start up invul. Air unblockable when used on ground. Air-usable.
Semblance: Rage:
When Yang’s health drops below 35%, she enters her empowered state, complete with flaming hair. While in this state, many of Yang's moves become buffed significantly. Most notably, all of Yang's punch normals and some of her special moves gain an extra hit(s) or/and frame advantages. While these hits are still affected by damage scaling, these hits do not add on any additional damage scaling to Yang's combos.
